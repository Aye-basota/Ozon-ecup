# Ensembles and lineage

- `SOLUTION_ANCESTRY.md` is the human navigation graph.
- `ancestry_edges.csv/jsonl` is the complete directed edge registry.
- Confirmed submission recipes are in `../leaderboard/chronology.csv`.
- All potential OOF/test members, including incomplete pairs, are in
  `../registry/components.csv`.

These files describe historical compositions only. They contain no newly tuned
weights and make no recommendation for a future ensemble.
